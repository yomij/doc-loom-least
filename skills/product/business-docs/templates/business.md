# Business document template

Adapt sections to the topic; omit unused sections. Apply the audience and
evidence rules in SKILL.md. Replace illustrative source IDs with actual sources.
Adapt this template to the user's language unless another is requested.

~~~md
# Business topic

- Business scope and applicable period/version:
- Preparation date:
- Document status: Draft / Archived (archival date and basis)
- Document type: Requirements discussion summary / Task business archive / Historical business logic review
- Related tasks, existing rules, or archives: (if any)
- Usage note: Archival preserves this summary; it does not imply rule approval, implementation, or current authority.

## Business goals and scope

Describe the background, problem, affected people, intended outcomes, and scope boundaries.

## Business processes and rules

For each business scenario, describe actors, conditions, actions, outcomes, and exceptions, with sources.
Distinguish confirmed requirements, observed behavior, and open questions; use headings or numbers for rules that need later references.

## Key decisions and requirement changes

Record significant choices, known reasons, and sources; for changes, state the original requirement, final decision, and affected rules.

## Execution or delivery status

Include only when relevant; mark delivery or verification as unknown or unverified when evidence is missing.

| Scenario | Expected business outcome and source | Actual delivery status | Verification status and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

## Open issues and implications

Unresolved questions, evidence gaps, conflicting rules, and scenarios not currently covered.

## Sources and verification notes

- S1: Requirements documents, identified conversation excerpts/summaries, or existing business archives.
- S2: Links and technical identifiers for implementation, test results, or historical records, with versions and evidence limitations.

## Subsequent changes

Link subsequent archives, change dates, and specific affected rules; preserve the archived body.
~~~
